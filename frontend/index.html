Accumulator settings

Survival-focused: only enters when ADX reads calm/ranging (lower = safer), cashes out automatically once take-profit is hit, and force-sells as a backstop if a trade runs past the max hold time. Set target ticks instead of a flat percentage to cash out faster and more reliably, at the cost of a smaller win (0 = use the percentage field instead). Candles are custom-built from raw ticks (Deriv's own bottom out at 60s) purely to compute ADX - lower = catches faster moves, but noisier.

Digit Differ settings

Fixed-odds, not directional: bets the next tick's last digit won't be one of N excluded digits. Win probability is exactly N/10, guaranteed by the contract itself - not a market read, so which digits get excluded doesn't matter (picked by simple rotation). More excluded digits means a higher win rate but a smaller payout per win - same skew as every other contract here, just shaped differently. Stake is split evenly across the N simultaneous bets, all placed over one connection.

Hybrid

Reads ADX on your chosen symbol each run and picks a style automatically: calm → Accumulator (survival mode), anything else → Digit Differ (fixed-odds, doesn't care about market conditions). Uses whatever Accumulator and Digit Differ are already configured to (switch to each tab to tune them) - just set your stake and risk limits below and it runs on its own. Tracked as its own strategy with its own stats, separate from running each style manually.

Risk management

Stake automatically scales down as you approach this - 75% size at 50% progress, 50% size at 80% progress. Never scales up. Trading pauses entirely once the goal is hit, resets Monday.
